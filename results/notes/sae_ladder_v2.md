# Abstraction ladder v2 — layers, a broad reference corpus, and the abstraction flow

Extends hour 15 (`scripts/sae_ladder.py`, `results/sae_ladder_picard.json`) in three bounded ways.
All numbers from `scripts/sae_ladder_v2.py` → `results/sae_ladder_v2.json`, figure
`results/figures/sae_ladder_v2.png`.

**Setup.** Gemma-2-9B-it, per-token residuals via NDIF. Gemma Scope JumpReLU dictionaries
(`pre = x@W_enc + b_enc; act = pre*(pre>threshold); x̂ = act@W_dec + b_dec`). Targets and references
as in hour 15: the canonical Picard description plus the 72 narrative passages (theme grid 4 themes ×
3 eras × 3 beats, mood grid 4 themes × 3 eras × 3 moods). New data: block-9 and block-31 residuals
for the same 73 texts (`scripts/ndif_tokens_resume.py`, a retrying/checkpointing variant of
`ndif_tokens.py` — plain `ndif_tokens.py` lost two runs to hung NDIF jobs), the 16k dictionaries at
layers 9 and 31, and a **broad reference corpus** of 111 passages across 12 genres (news, dialogue,
technical prose, recipe, legal, poetry, sports, science explanation, personal email, product
description, popular history, how-to) generated by Gemma-2-9B-it itself
(`scripts/ndif_gen_broad.py` → `results/broad_corpus.json`) with block-20 residuals fetched the same
way. Generality of a feature = fraction of reference passages on which it fires on ≥1 token;
"content features" = those whose peak token on the target is alphabetic and whose generality ≤ 0.9
(the hour-15 filter).

## 1. More layers: width moves generality, depth does not

Width 16k, 72 narrative references, Picard description as target.

| layer | dictionary | content features | mean generality | median | rare (<0.1) | >0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9  | `layer_9/width_16k/average_l0_47`  | 1779 / 2041 | 0.197 | 0.111 | 0.49 | 0.12 |
| 20 | `layer_20/width_16k/average_l0_47` | 1404 / 1898 | **0.182** | 0.111 | 0.48 | 0.08 |
| 31 | `layer_31/width_16k/average_l0_43` | 1310 / 1626 | 0.200 | 0.125 | 0.45 | 0.10 |
| 20, width 131k (hour 15) | `average_l0_43` | 1500 | **0.063** | 0.014 | 0.83 | — |

The distribution is flat across depth (0.18–0.20, rare 0.45–0.49) while the 16k→131k width step at
one layer moves it by 3×. **Dictionary width, not depth, is the axis that carries the abstraction
ladder** — at least by this label-free measure.

What *does* change with depth is what the strongest features are anchored on. Layer 9's top feature
is the Star Trek particular itself (`Federation / fleet / Enterprise / Picard`, generality 0.31) and
`Earl Grey / tea / hot` survives at 0.17; layer 31's top features are relational and near-universal
(`which / an / Federation / no` at 0.35, `and / no / with / which` at 0.72, `him / young / mentors`
at 0.85). So depth abstracts by changing the *kind* of feature, width by changing the *generality of
the same kind*. Layer 20 sits between, with `stars / Federation / Star` at 0.38 — the hour-15 rung.

## 2. Broad reference corpus: the width effect survives, halved

At layer 20, generality recomputed against the 111 broad passages instead of the 72 narrative ones.

| dictionary | mean generality (narrative, hour 15 replication) | mean generality (broad) | rare narrative → broad |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16k | 0.182 | **0.187** | 0.48 → 0.50 |
| 131k | 0.063 | **0.101** | 0.83 → 0.80 |

(The narrative column reproduces hour 15's 0.18 vs 0.06.) Against the broad corpus the 16k number is
unchanged and the 131k number rises by 60%, so the ratio drops from **2.9× to 1.9×** — still a clear
gap, but part of hour 15's effect was an artefact of a narrow reference set: fine 131k features
anchored on non-narrative material (formatting, instructional register, product vocabulary) look
"rare" against 72 story passages and are merely off-domain, not fine-grained.

**Merge test.** For the 15 strongest 131k content features on the target, the nearest 16k feature by
decoder cosine is more general in **13 of 15** under the broad corpus (13/15 under the narrative one
here; hour 15 reported 12/15 — the difference is the slightly different content filter, not new
data). The clean cases are unchanged in character:

- `Enterprise / the / Picard` (131k, generality **0.00**) → `stars / the / Federation` (16k, **0.01**), cos 0.45 — the Star Trek particular has no broad home at either width, but the narrow feature is the space-setting one.
- `Enterprise / -ners / Federation` (0.80) → `Federation / -ners / Enterprise` (0.82), cos 1.00 — a near-identity pair: this is a *format/position* feature that merely happens to peak on those tokens.
- `Grey / quotes / He` (0.02) → `principle / , / Earl` (0.07), cos 0.27 — the weak, low-cosine match for the idiosyncratic particular, exactly as in hour 15.
- `force / than / loses` (0.34) → `force / A / than` (**1.00**), cos 0.89 — under the broad corpus several narrow matches jump to generality 1.0 because the narrow feature is partly a position feature (see §3).

## 3. The abstraction flow and the fixed-point test

Abstraction level k at layer 20, width 16k: keep only features with generality ≥ g_k, reconstruct
each of the 72 narrative passages' mean residual from the survivors (`x̂ = Σ_{f∈S} ā_f W_dec[f] +
b_dec`), and measure pairwise structure. Generality measured against the **broad** corpus (the
narrative-corpus version is in the JSON and behaves the same, more slowly). Labels: theme = the
8 story subjects (debt, message, door, meal, gate, theft, farewell, storm), era = medieval / 1920s /
farfuture. 8-NN purity is the mean fraction of a passage's 8 nearest neighbours sharing its label
after mean-centering (chance 8/71 = 0.11 for theme, 23/71 = 0.32 for era).

| g_k | features | act. mass kept | mean pairwise cos distance | euclid. spread / ‖x̂‖ | 8-NN theme | 8-NN era | within/across theme | within/across era |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw residual (no SAE) | — | — | 0.0548 | 0.337 | 0.65 | 0.54 | 0.66 | 0.89 |
| 0.0 (full 16k) | 16384 | 1.00 | 0.0268 | 0.240 | 0.63 | 0.53 | 0.61 | 0.89 |
| 0.01 | 9897 | 0.94 | 0.0235 | 0.226 | **0.64** | 0.48 | 0.59 | 0.92 |
| 0.02 | 8318 | 0.89 | 0.0207 | 0.213 | **0.64** | 0.47 | 0.58 | 0.93 |
| 0.05 | 5426 | 0.77 | 0.0156 | 0.188 | 0.60 | 0.47 | 0.56 | 0.93 |
| 0.1 | 2681 | 0.57 | 0.0087 | 0.146 | 0.52 | 0.48 | 0.52 | 0.92 |
| 0.2 | 1107 | 0.40 | 0.0056 | 0.123 | 0.43 | 0.48 | 0.48 | 0.92 |
| 0.4 | 415 | 0.30 | 0.0044 | 0.112 | 0.42 | 0.41 | 0.45 | 0.95 |
| 0.7 | 172 | 0.24 | 0.0030 | 0.098 | 0.36 | 0.36 | 0.42 | 0.96 |
| 0.95 | 58 | 0.17 | 0.0011 | 0.076 | 0.30 | 0.39 | 0.38 | 0.96 |

**(a) Distances collapse, monotonically and without a plateau.** Mean pairwise cosine distance falls
24× from 0.0268 to 0.0011; the scale-free Euclidean spread falls 3.2× (0.240 → 0.076). There is no
level at which the 72 passages sit at a few well-separated attractors: the flow runs smoothly into
one point. Note the ladder already starts below the raw residual (0.055 → 0.027): re-encoding
through a 16k dictionary is itself the first rung.

**(b) Toward one blob, not toward theme/era.** 8-NN theme purity falls from 0.63–0.64 to 0.30 and era
from 0.53 to 0.36–0.39 (era's chance floor is 0.32, so era structure is essentially gone by g_k =
0.7). Any intermediate optimum is marginal: theme purity peaks at g_k = 0.01–0.02 (0.64) — one point
above the unfiltered dictionary and below the raw residual's 0.65 — and then decays monotonically.
Era decays *first and faster* (relative within/across ratio 0.89 → 0.96, i.e. within-era pairs become
no closer than across-era pairs), which is the one piece of RG-flavoured sorting that does show up:
**era is the irrelevant direction and dies early, theme is the more relevant one and survives
further, but neither is preserved at the top**. The mean-level theme contrast (within/across ratio
0.61 → 0.38) keeps improving while the local neighbourhoods degrade; that is what a shrinking cloud
with one surviving coarse theme axis looks like, not a set of archetype attractors.

**(c) The survivors at the top are trivially general.** The 58 features with generality ≥ 0.95,
ranked by total activation across the corpus, are anchored on:

| feature | generality | top tokens across the 72 passages |
|---|---|---|
| f10439, f12492, f2727, f7700, f16288, f8682, f8508, f14601, f358, f7223 | 1.00 | `A A A A A A` (the passage-initial article — a position/format feature) |
| f15483 | 1.00 | `. . . . . .` (sentence boundary) |
| f3375 | 0.96 | ` the`, ` for`, ` up`, ` so`, ` was`, ` do` (function words) |
| f7851 | 1.00 | ` afternoon`, ` broke`, ` read`, ` tradition`, ` sounded`, ` ran` (past-tense narrative verbs) |
| f771 | 1.00 | ` plowing`, ` had`, ` morning`, ` heart`, ` changed`, ` born` |
| f10480 | 0.98 | ` came`, ` came`, ` came`, ` was`, ` was`, ` was` |

Ten of the top sixteen are the same first-token/position feature split many ways; the rest are
sentence punctuation, function words and a generic past-tense narrative-verb feature. No archetype,
no hero's journey, nothing that reads as a universality class of stories.

## Verdict on the corrected expectation

VISION.md's corrected note (the coarse-graining section, following the groovy-commutator observation
that RG fixed points are the affine/absorbing rules and the interesting class lives between them)
predicted two things. The first is **confirmed, clearly**: the fixed points of the abstraction flow
are trivially general — passage-initial formatting, sentence boundaries, function words and generic
past-tense verbs — and the distance between passages collapses toward one blob rather than onto a
few archetypes. L7's "fixed points (archetypes as attractors)" should be marked **falsified in this
realization**, not merely conjectured.

The second half — "theme/era separation is best at an intermediate level, i.e. in the flow" — is
**not** confirmed. Separation is best at the *bottom* of the ladder (g_k ≤ 0.02, and the raw residual
is better still) and decays monotonically upward; the peak at g_k = 0.01–0.02 is within noise of the
unfiltered dictionary. What lives in the flow is not an optimum but an *ordering*: era's structure
dies before theme's, which is the relevant/irrelevant sorting L7 also asks for, and that part
survives. So: the quotient story stands (§2: narrower keeps more general, 13/15 merges up, now
against a genre-broad corpus), the RG *flow* has a legible slope, and the RG *fixed points* are
empty of narrative content.

## Caveats

- Generality is label-free co-occurrence, not a feature label; "trivially general" is read off top
  tokens, not off Neuronpedia (still unreachable).
- One target description, one model, one width per layer outside layer 20; the 131k dictionary exists
  only at layer 20 here (disk), so the width × depth cell is unfilled.
- The broad corpus is model-generated by the same model being probed, and genre-imbalanced
  (email 6, legal 8, poetry 8, the rest 9–10 of 111).
- The flow thresholds features by generality and reconstructs; it does not renormalise the
  reconstruction's norm, so the raw cosine collapse and the Euclidean spread are reported separately.
- Theme/era labels come from the same authored grids as everything upstream; the collapse verdict is
  about those two axes only.
